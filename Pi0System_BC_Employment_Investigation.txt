Epi0nGpi0n-Led BC Employment Market Investigation (2024-2049)


Epi0nGpi0n, leading the Pi0System, has conducted a comprehensive investigation into the British Columbia (BC) employment market. The goal: design adaptive 2-year, 5-year, 10-year, and 25-year rolling plans to maintain and expand employment, foster new revenue streams, and ensure positive ROI for both the BC government and employers. The plan emphasizes:
- Adaptive, future-proof employment strategies
- Sectoral expansion through targeted training and integration
- Value-added manufacturing and shipping (not just raw materials)
- Cross-border collaboration (Alberta, US West Coast, Asia, Americas)
- Government incentives for job creation and business expansion

5-YEAR GOVERNMENT INCENTIVE PLAN:

- Direct wage subsidies for employers creating new jobs in value-added sectors
- Tax credits for training/upskilling programs tied to export growth
- Grants for BC-Alberta joint processing ventures
- Export support for finished goods (shipping, marketing, trade missions)
- Infrastructure investment in advanced manufacturing and green energy
- Support for digital/AI/tech talent retention and attraction
- Special incentives for businesses expanding exports to Asia, US, Mexico, Central/South America
